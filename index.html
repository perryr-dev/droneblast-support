Gameplay Help

Aim your turret and hold to fire. Intercept carriers before they release their drones and keep your command base standing.

Watch your turret heat and use EMP and flak when the sky gets crowded. For a refresher, open How to Play / Enemy Guide from the main menu.

Premium Purchases

Premium is an optional one-time purchase that removes ads between runs and unlocks additional scenery and turret finishes.

To restore Premium, open Options → Premium / Appearance and select Restore Purchase. Use the same store account you used for the original purchase.

Report a Problem

When contacting support, please include:

  • Your device model and operating system version
  • Your Drone Blast! game version
  • What happened and the steps to reproduce it
  • A screenshot, if helpful

Please do not send passwords or payment card details.
